# Responsible AI and traditional-knowledge stewardship

KAPPA is a hypothesis-generation research prototype. It does not recommend treatments or predict individual responses. The UI separates measured assay values, source-level botanical occurrence, a secondary ethnobotanical report, chemical identity, model outputs and pharmacogenomic guideline evidence. No computational inference is presented as an established mechanism, efficacy claim or clinical result.

The single turmeric-use item is attributed to a 2012 secondary paper that itself cites a Malayalam-language book not independently accessed. The paper does not identify a specific community/locality for the table entry. KAPPA does not infer that context, claim safety/efficacy, or connect the claim mechanistically to curcumin/PTGS2. The paper's reuse licence was not independently verified. Traditional knowledge is not public domain by default; any future source-specific ingestion requires rights and access review and, where applicable, community provenance, consent and cultural stewardship. Do not publish restricted knowledge merely because it appears in a scraped or published source.

The CPIC CYP2C19–clopidogrel example is a separate public guideline evidence demonstration, not a KAPPA pharmacogenomic prediction. It is filtered to a documented phenotype/population context. ClinVar is used only for molecular identity, not response classification. There is no patient genotype or curcumin/PTGS2 PGx edge. No ancestry-specific, population-level or precision-medicine effectiveness is claimed. Any future population association work would require careful study design, allele/diplotype context, linkage, ancestry, phenotype and causal interpretation. Missing associations are not evidence of no relationship.

Model outputs are uncalibrated, limited to a single biochemical endpoint, and are not treated as clinical probabilities. Similarity is an applicability diagnostic only; the empirical threshold is not a guarantee. External validation has not been performed. Independent assay evaluation and human expert review are prerequisites for stronger claims.

AI-assisted development through Arena.ai Agent Mode contributed to code, documentation and presentation drafting/editing. Researchers remain responsible for verification of results, citations, licensing and claims; see `submission/third_party_disclosures.md`. The running application uses no external generative-AI inference. Third-party software and datasets retain their original licenses.
