# Result files

- `model_metrics.json`: executed scaffold holdout/CV, model comparison, training-only hyperparameter sensitivity, calibration diagnostics, applicability reference, grouped permutation importance and internal stress tests.
- `data_quality.json`: raw/cross-filter sequential audit and missingness summary.
- `curcumin_demo_assessment.json`: exact training structure/assay match and model-only uncalibrated retrospective response; no composite weight sensitivity.
- Figures in `../figures/` are regenerated by `python scripts/make_figures.py`; the ML-training figures are also generated by `src/ml/train.py`.

Metrics and demo outputs are run-specific and should not be described as external or prospective validation.
