5. **Model lab (35 s):** show ROC/PR plus balanced accuracy, calibration and scaffold-CV. Mention the document-held-out ROC-AUC 0.724 and temporal/scaffold-disjoint 0.602 are internal ChEMBL stress tests, not external validation.
6. **Evidence graph (25 s):** switch between plant/assay and pharmacogenomics views. In the PGx view, show CYP2C19 *2 / rs4244285 identity, clopidogrel and CPIC context. State ClinVar supplies an identity cross-reference only (its cached 'Likely benign; other' germline label concerns a not-specified disease trait, not drug metabolism); recommendation text is CPIC-filtered to the displayed phenotype and ACS/PCI population. There is no link to curcumin/PTGS2 and no patient genotype.
7. **Close (15 s):** show methods/provenance and source licences. KAPPA is a research prototype for testable hypotheses—not efficacy evidence, medical advice or a treatment recommender.

Avoid: calling curcumin a hit; interpreting a model output as potency or likelihood of therapeutic success; implying the traditional-use report establishes efficacy; or connecting CYP2C19–clopidogrel evidence to the plant model.
